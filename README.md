# College-Football-Threat-Value

This is a project I've been working on in an attempt to both extend my knowledge of C and R as a programmer, and to overcome bland and vague metrics in Sports Analytics. Using critical data about a team, I use various equations to weigh each team's Explosivity against how much they threatened to score (Threat Value).

The higher on the X-axis a team is, the more they threatened to score and generally dominated the game from an offense perspective. Defense and Special Teams play a minor role in Threat Value.

The higher on the Y-axis a team is, the more explosive they were as an offense. Defensive touchdowns will greatly increase a team's explosivity rating, as explosivity is based on scoring as much as possible in few drives, plays, and time. This is intentionally baked-in so that teams with high-scoring defenses will be factored in as well.

I plan to chart every Power 4 conference team's games this season and I am excited to see if this experiment pays off in the form of new insight into football.
